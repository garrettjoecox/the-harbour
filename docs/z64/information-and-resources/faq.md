# F.A.Q.

## Where can I get a ROM?
In order to legally acquire a ROM compatible with either SoH or 2Ship you need to dump a supported Gamecube disc or N64 cartridge. For more details please consult the [ROM dumping guide](../common/rom-dumping-guide). Please note that piracy is illegal and is not supported or tolerated by the Harbor Masters team.

## Is this legal?
Yes! The Ship of Harkinian and 2Ship2Harkinian PC ports do not contain any copyrighted material and require players to legally acquire the appropriate game ROMs and therefore are perfectly clean.

## What's with the names?
Ship of Harkinian is a play on the Ship of Theseus thought experiment which questions whether an object that has had all of its original components replaced is truly the same object. The Harkinian part comes from King Harkinian from the Zelda CDI games. The design of the ship on the SoH logo is also meant to resemble King Harkinian's ship from those games.
The name 2Ship2Harkinian is a reference to the naming schemes of the Fast and Furious movies.

SoH major releases are named after sci-fi film characters from 1982 (apparently Ackbar first appeared in a comic in 1982 which is why he still counts despite his film debut being in 1983).
The 2Ship releases are said to be named after characters related to time travel but so far they all seem to be from "Higurashi: When They Cry" (which as far as I can tell do not feature time travel but I could be wrong lol).

## Why aren't ETAs given?
This project is a labor of love crafted by passionate volunteers who dedicate their spare time to making it happen without any compensation. The workflow is largely flexible, allowing individuals to work on the tasks that they find most interesting, with a few key milestones that are prioritized for stable releases. By avoiding strict deadlines, we prevent the creation of unrealistic expectations that would only lead to disappointment, and most of the time, the devs themselves don't know until just before something happens.

## Can I Load existing ROM Hacks?
No. Since Ship of Harkinian and 2Ship2Harkinian are ports, not emulators, they do not run ROMs. ROMs are only used by the ports in order to extract game assets.
If any romhacks get support for Ship of Harkinian in the future, it will be done by the romhack creators recoding the hacks into SoH's codebase
